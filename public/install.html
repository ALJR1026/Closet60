Add Closet60 to your home screen

No app store needed — it takes about 10 seconds and works just like an installed app. Pick your phone below.

1
Open Closet60 in Safari or Chrome, then tap the Share icon in the address bar.
2
Scroll down the share sheet and tap “Add to Home Screen.”
3
Tap Add to confirm. Closet60 now sits on your home screen like any other app.
1
Open Closet60 in Chrome, then tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
2
Tap “Install app” (some versions show “Add to Home screen” instead).
3
Tap Install to confirm. Closet60 now sits on your home screen and app drawer.

Exact wording and icon placement can vary slightly between phone models and browser versions — but "Add to Home Screen" / "Install app" is always the option you're looking for.
